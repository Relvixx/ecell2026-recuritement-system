# Recruitment 2026 Media Mapping

## Raw-media audit

The `MEDIA_RAW` source folder was audited without modifying its contents. It contains 17 JPEGs and one 57.47-second, 1080 × 1920 portrait HEVC MOV. The strongest material is the planning/workshop coverage, E-CELL-branded team photograph, stage recognition, and event celebration photography.

The collection has several low-resolution or repetitive group shots. The 542 × 327 group image, 802 × 323 group image, informal food image, and repeated classroom scenes were not used. No source is reused in the final casting.

## Video decision

`IMG_8033.MOV` is a 90.3 MB, 57.47-second portrait HEVC video. The existing Life component has a poster slot only and no approved playback controls, so the video itself is deliberately not integrated and no production MP4 was produced. The 00:42 frame is used only as the optimized WebP poster.

## Output treatment

All selected images are WebP derivatives made from the raw originals. Their source dimensions are retained except for the intentional wide video-poster crop. Focal positions are stored with the production media map and applied with `object-fit: cover`; no raw source was destructively cropped or altered.

# 9. FAQ

### Do I need previous experience?
No. Previous experience can help, but it isn't the only thing we're looking for. Curiosity, effort, thinking and willingness to learn matter too.

### Can first-year students apply?
Yes. Recruitment is open to 1st, 2nd and 3rd-year students.

### Can I choose two teams?
Yes. You'll choose one primary team and can optionally select a second preference.

### What if I'm not sure which team fits me?
Explore the team descriptions first and choose the kind of work you'd genuinely enjoy trying. You don't need to have everything figured out already.

### What happens after I submit?
We'll review your application. If you're shortlisted, you'll receive details about the next interaction or interview stage.

### How much time will E-CELL require?
It depends on the team and what's happening that week. During events, things can get busier. In the application, we'll ask how much time you can realistically contribute.

### Can I edit my application after submitting?
Please review your application carefully before submitting. If something important needs correcting later, contact the recruitment team.

### How will I know my status?
After submitting, you'll receive an Application ID that you can use with your email to track your current recruitment status.
